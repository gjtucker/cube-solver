What do the letters mean?

Each letter is a face, seen from how you're holding the cube: Up · Down · Front · Back · Right · Left.

A plain letter (R) = turn that face a quarter turn clockwise while looking straight at it. An apostrophe (R') = counter-clockwise. A two (R2) = half turn.

On the 4×4, a lowercase letter (r) means the inner slice next to that face — just the single inside layer, not the outer face.

On the 2×2 and mirror cube, solutions never turn the Back or Left faces — the back-bottom-left piece stays still, so use it as your anchor.
